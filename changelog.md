# Changelog

Every future feature or behavior change must add a brief one-line entry here.

## 2026-10-17

- Faster insights — Loads independent insight sections concurrently so quick results appear first.

## 2026-10-16

- Unusual sessions — Finds listening sessions that differ sharply from normal habits.
- Listening extras — Adds artist A–Z, title moods, one-hit wonders, telepathy, and most-in-sync days.
- Rediscover roulette — Picks a formerly loved song that has not been played for over a year.
- Which Came First — Challenges players to order songs by when they first heard them.

## 2026-10-14

- Versus mode — Compares two people's history with one artist across songs, totals, streaks, and timelines.

## 2026-10-12

- Concert preparation — Tracks concerts and builds spoiler-free prep lists from listening history and popular songs.
- Expandable insight lists — Reveals additional results without overwhelming the initial view.
- One-song cap — Limits rankings to one song per album or artist.
- Artist focus — Restricts all statistics to a selected group of artists.

## 2026-10-11

- Album insights — Shows complete listens, start-to-finish sessions, deep cuts, one-song albums, and ownership twists.
- Hidden artists — Excludes selected artists from all rankings and statistics.
- Insight prefetching — Loads insight data in the background so the panel opens faster.

## 2026-10-10

- Name That Tune improvements — Adds selectable top-song pools and avoids recently repeated clips.
- Name That Tune — Guesses songs from progressively longer preview clips.
- Song Bracket — Pits top songs against each other until one winner remains.
- Guess the Month — Identifies a month from its leading songs.
- Listening sessions — Summarizes session lengths, openers, closers, and longest sessions.
- Calendar habits — Breaks listening down by season, weekday, Friday night, and Christmas activity.
- Playback habits — Compares chosen, queued, shuffled, and autoplayed music across devices.
- Travel listening — Highlights music played away from the usual location.
- Love-hate songs — Finds frequently played songs that are also skipped often.
- Music profile — Summarizes genres, decades, nostalgia, song lengths, and listening milestones.
- Awards night — Turns standout listening statistics into shareable awards.
- Around the clock — Shows hourly listening and signature music for each three-hour daypart.
- Time travel — Finds comebacks, steady favorites, flings, slow burners, anniversaries, and who discovered songs first.
- Listening personality — Adds badges, biggest days, top music, streaks, and period-by-period eras.
- Recaps — Presents personal or shared listening highlights as an animated slideshow.
- Higher or Lower — Guesses whether one song has more plays than another.
- Who Played It More — Guesses which person listened to a shared song more.
- Apple Music import — Reads Apple Music activity, library, and daily-history exports alongside Spotify and Tidal.

## 2026-10-05

- Insight dashboard — Adds taste compatibility, streaks, listening-time heatmaps, and location summaries.
- Detail views — Shows timelines, per-person totals, lyrics, and top tracks for songs, artists, and albums.
- Discovery views — Adds rediscovery and person-difference rankings with skip and trend sorting.
- Playlist copy — Copies the current top 50 songs as a playlist-ready text list.

## 2026-10-04

- Multiple people — Imports separate histories and compares them with filters, weights, and shared-score formulas.

## 2026-09-28

- Listening dashboard — Imports Spotify and Tidal history and ranks songs with search, date, service, and duration filters.
- Artwork and lyrics — Looks up and caches cover images and song lyrics for detail viewing.
- Decay scoring — Weights recent listening more heavily with a configurable half-life.
